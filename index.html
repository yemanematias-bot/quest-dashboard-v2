FIELD GUIDE

HOW THE SYSTEM WORKS

Everything needed to operate Quest Dashboard V4 without guessing.

?
01

Choose your mission

Main is for major objectives. Daily builds habits. Side holds optional tasks. Weekly tracks repeated targets.

02

Forge a quest

Open a quest screen and press Add. Choose its name, XP reward, difficulty and the character attribute it develops.

03

Set the priority

Use ☆ Pin on one Main Quest. It becomes your active directive and appears on the Overview screen.

04

Complete and level up

Completing missions awards XP and attributes. Every 1,000 XP increases your level and unlocks stronger titles.

05

Build streaks

Daily Quests reset each day. Completing them on consecutive days increases their streak and feeds linked Weekly Trials.

06

Protect progress

Sign in for cloud sync across devices. Export a backup from History before major changes. Local progress still works offline.

COMMON QUESTIONS

FAQ

Which quest type should I use?

Main Quests are major outcomes; Daily Quests repeat every day; Side Quests are one-off optional tasks; Weekly Trials require several completions before claiming XP.

What happens when I complete a quest?

You receive its XP, your completed-quest total increases and its chosen character attribute gains points. One-off quests then move into History.

How do Weekly Trials update automatically?

When creating or editing a Weekly Trial, link it to a Daily Quest. Each completion of that Daily automatically adds one Weekly progress point.

Where is my progress stored?

It is stored locally in your browser. When signed in, the complete save is also synced to your private cloud account.

Can somebody else use my link?

Yes. Everyone gets separate local progress. They should create their own account for cloud sync and never use another person's login.

What should I do if the dashboard looks outdated?

Force-refresh with Ctrl + F5 on desktop. On mobile, close the tab and reopen it after GitHub Pages finishes deploying.
